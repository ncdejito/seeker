Anti-illegal recruiter. Anyone in the Philippines who recruits you for a job abroad must appear on the DMW licensed-agency list. Never pay a “guaranteed visa”, never surrender your passport, never fly as a tourist “to convert to work”. Report tips to airtipinfo@dmw.gov.ph or DMW 1348. Visa notes on this page are a map — verify on the official site.
