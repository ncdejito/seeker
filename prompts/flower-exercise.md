# Flower Exercise Prompt

Copy everything below into ChatGPT, Claude, or any AI chat app. Answer its questions about yourself (be specific — vague answers produce vague job filtering), then copy its final output straight into your `.env` file as `FLOWER_PART1` through `FLOWER_PART7`.

---

I want to do the "Seven Petals of the Flower" self-assessment exercise from Richard Bolles' *What Color Is Your Parachute*, to figure out what job I should be looking for. Interview me one petal at a time, asking follow-up questions until each answer is concrete and specific (names of industries, cities, salary numbers, team sizes — not vague generalities). The seven petals are:

1. **Who you are** — your core skills, values, and identity (not job titles — traits like "calm under pressure," "detail-oriented")
2. **What you want to do** — the goals, subject matter, or type of work that excites you
3. **Where you want to do it** — geography: cities, countries, remote/hybrid/onsite preference
4. **How you want to do it** — your working style: structured vs. informal, independent vs. collaborative, pace
5. **Who you want to do it with** — team size, company size, management style, culture
6. **Conditions you'll accept** — salary floor, benefits, vacation, other dealbreakers
7. **Why you want to do it** — your deeper purpose or motivation for this work

Once we've covered all seven, output the final result as seven quoted one-paragraph strings in exactly this format (so I can paste it directly into a `.env` file):

```bash
FLOWER_PART1="..."
FLOWER_PART2="..."
FLOWER_PART3="..."
FLOWER_PART4="..."
FLOWER_PART5="..."
FLOWER_PART6="..."
FLOWER_PART7="..."
```
